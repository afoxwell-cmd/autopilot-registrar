Autopilot Registrar

Sign in with your work account, scan the QR on the device, and it registers into your organization's Intune.
Step 1: sign in with your Microsoft 365 work account. The registration is performed under your identity - the MSP never holds your tenant's credentials.
Requires: Intune Administrator role in your tenant, and one-time admin consent for the app.
Step 2: scan the device's QR code
On the new device in OOBE: press the Windows key 5 times → Autopilot menu → assign device association. Hold your phone over the QR.
Step 3: confirm the device
Why this design is safe
• Your credentials, your tenant: the app signs you in with your own work account and calls Microsoft Graph with a delegated token. The MSP holds no secrets for your tenant and cannot act without a signed-in authorized user.
• Auditable: every registration appears in your Entra ID sign-in logs and Intune audit logs under the name of the person who scanned.
• Revoke anytime: your admin can revoke consent or block the app in Entra ID enterprise applications.
• TPM-backed: the QR identity is only half of the proof - the physical device must present its TPM private key during OOBE, so a copied payload is useless.
